# October 9, 2026 correctness fixes

Review baseline: `0b2cb7950e59a220bfa023b214fafa3eecf41ff6`.
The baseline's 1,584 tests passed despite the following reproduced defects.
The fixes follow the repository's curated references and existing user
adjudications; no new house rules were introduced.

| # | Finding | Correction and regression coverage |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rejected actions consumed resources | Dispatcher restores the board, nested references, RNG, and history on failure. March and Wastage retry tests cover the contract. |
| 2 | Hidden cards, plans, and future draws leaked | Player views redact private history, both draw piles, RNG inputs, and midgame opponent reasoning. Own choices and publicly revealed cards remain visible. |
| 3 | Three casualty-free rounds awarded defender victory | Battle continues until Rout or Concede, following D-Q011. A computation limit cannot award victory. Bounded previews report incomplete trials separately and compute outcome statistics from completed battles. |
| 4 | Routed Storm units struck later in the round | Every Strike is recalculated from surviving units at its initiative step (4.4.2/4.5.2). |
| 5 | Identical saved states produced different casualties across processes | Target ordering is stable before consuming seeded dice. Regression compares independent Python hash seeds. |
| 6 | Mandatory Arts of War draws could be skipped | Advance requires the once-per-Levy draw and completion of pending implementation/choices (3.1). Exhausted draw piles can still complete the draw action. |
| 7 | Enemy-held sources supplied armies | Blockade validation covers every route locale, including endpoints (4.6.2). |
| 8 | Ships carried Supply inland without land transport | Ship sources require both the Ships and compatible transport on each intervening Way (4.6.2/4.6.3). |
| 9 | Wrong attacking Lord led a Storm | The active Commanding Lord is placed first in the Storm Array (4.5.2). |
| 10 | Garrison Armor ignored Crossbows | Crossbow identity follows each Hit through Walls, Garrison, and Lord absorption; Garrison Armor receives the penalty. |
| 11 | Cogs did not double Supply Ships | Supply applies usable Ship capacity and Cogs sharing. |
| 12 | Additional Seats supplied nothing | Supply preserves Seat multiplicity, including R15 Novgorod and T12 Wenden, within the two-Seat limit. |
| 13 | Tempest rounded Cogs losses down | Remove half the Ships rounded up, as R16 specifies. |
| 14 | Swedish Crusade omitted a target with mixed marker types | Resolve both existing Calendar markers; partial/empty target selections cannot bypass the Event. |
| 15 | Russians chose Osilian Revolt's Teutonic target | Reveal R9, pause for the Teutonic choice, resolve, and resume Russian Arts of War. The pending choice survives saving/loading. |
| 16 | Batu Khan rejected zero shift | R10 accepts zero as required by its text and Q-R201-A. |
| 17 | Torzhok losses could be bypassed | A supplied asset order must remove three Assets, or all available when fewer than three exist. |
| 18 | Sally and Rearguard survivors could stop engaging | Follow-through testing exposed missing row transitions previously hidden by the early-victory guard. When the main attacking Front is gone, surviving Sally and Rearguard forces become opposing Fronts and eligible Reserves advance (4.4.2). |
